What's new in RunOut

Tip: you can reopen this any time from the "Changes" link in the footer.

v38 — May 2026

  • Dark mode with a toggle in the header. The choice is remembered across sessions and follows your system preference on first visit.
  • Hide search results temporarily with the × in the top-right corner of any card — handy for narrowing down versions you've ruled out. A new search brings them all back.
  • Collection view: hide the entire filter column for full-width browsing, sort moved up next to List/Grid, sticky toolbar that stays in view as you scroll.
  • Filters in Search and Wantlist can now be collapsed with a "Hide filters" toggle, with a count badge so you don't lose track of active filters.

v29 — April 2026

  • Collection grid view with cover art and a track-list popup when you click a release.
  • Personal fields: purchase price, tags and private notes per release — encrypted and synced across devices.
  • Multi-select filters on Search, Collection and Wantlist (with a Size filter everywhere).
  • Infinite scroll instead of pagination, plus a Scroll-to-top button.
  • Full-width layout on desktop — more releases visible per screen on wide displays.
  • Hover a thumbnail in "Show all images" to get a larger floating preview.

v28 — April 2026

  • Barcode scanner in the native Android and iOS apps.
  • Expandable Notes & identifiers and Marketplace & prices sections per release.
  • Automatic username lookup — no longer required to paste it separately from the token.
